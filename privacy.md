# Privacy Policy for FlareTV

**Last Updated:** August 31, 2026

FlareTV ("we", "our", or "the Application") is a desktop TV launcher and media interface application. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use FlareTV.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect (Central Telemetry & Analytics)
* **No Telemetry or Tracking:** FlareTV does not include tracking pixels, behavioral analytics tools, or telemetry trackers.
* **No Centralized Servers:** We do not host central servers that harvest, store, or sell your personal data or viewing habits.

---

## 2. Information Handled Locally on Your Device
FlareTV operates primarily as a local application. The following data is processed and stored exclusively on your local device:

* **Application Settings & Preferences:** UI configurations, theme choices, display settings, and custom launcher shortcuts.
* **Local Media Paths & Cache:** Information regarding local file directories, media library indices, and temporary image/metadata caches.
* **Authentication Tokens & Credentials:** Any API keys, session tokens, or local server endpoints (e.g., Jellyfin, media server integrations, or tracking services) you configure are saved locally on your machine.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and External APIs
When enabled by you, FlareTV may communicate directly with third-party APIs or external services to fetch metadata, posters, or sync watch statuses. 

Depending on your configuration, these may include:
* **Media Metadata Providers:** (e.g., TVMaze, TMDB, AniList) to fetch show information, episode guides, and cover artwork.
* **Self-Hosted / Remote Media Servers:** Direct connections to servers you manage (e.g., Jellyfin).

**Please Note:** Interactions with third-party services are subject to their respective privacy policies and terms of service. FlareTV only transmits the minimum queries necessary to fulfill your metadata or streaming requests.

---

## 4. Data Security
Because all your configuration data, tokens, and preferences are stored locally on your device, the security of your data depends on your device's security and permissions. We recommend keeping your operating system up to date and securing your local network.

---

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect updates in the application or applicable laws. Any changes will be posted with an updated "Last Updated" date.

---

## 6. Contact
If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through the project repository or contact the developer directly.
